5.1 DBiz GPT Platform — Ingestion and Vector Stack Update
The CTO began with the completion of the Playwright web-loader rollout
(Action Item 47-02, closed 23 Aug). The CTO reported that Playwright
v1.60 had now replaced the legacy Cheerio + pdf.js based HTML ingestion
pipeline for all 12 document source types, with a measured improvement of:
  - 21.4% reduction in document processing time per 1,000 pages (from
    74 seconds to 58 seconds)
  - 37% reduction in chunking errors for JavaScript-rendered content
  - 14.2% improvement in RAG answer faithfulness scores for web-sourced
    documents in the internal dev QA harness

Environment variable PLAYWRIGHT_WS_URL=ws://playwright:3000 is now the
standard deployment configuration for all clusters, with 8 Playwright
worker pods in the primary SG cluster and 4 workers each in EU and US
standby clusters.

Vector database stack architecture (finalised after 4 months of testing
against Pinecone, Weaviate, and Qdrant alternatives):
  - PRIMARY: pgvector running on PostgreSQL 16 with IVFFlat indexes on
    2048-dimension BGE-M3 embeddings, as part of the primary OLTP
    database on AWS RDS Aurora Serverless v2 (PostgreSQL-compatible)
  - FALLBACK / EDGE: Chroma open-source vector store deployed on regional
    edge Kubernetes clusters, for low-latency EU and US queries
  - Approximately 200,000 chunks indexed across all tenants as of 11
    September 2026, growing at approximately 32,000 chunks/month
  - Hybrid search combining BM25 keyword matching with ANN approximate
    nearest-neighbour embedding search, weighted 0.35 BM25 / 0.65 ANN on
    general queries and adaptive weights per-query

5.2 Embeddings, Chunking and Reranking Stack
  - Embeddings Model: BAAI/bge-m3 (multilingual BGE-M3). This model was
    selected over alternatives (text-embedding-3-large, Voyage-Law-2,
    Cohere-embed-multilingual-v3) after a 6-week evaluation on the
    DBizLabs internal 12-document-type QA golden set. BGE-M3 scored
    highest on SG English + Tamil + Mandarin multilingual retrieval tasks
    and on cross-domain retrieval (Bond pricing + manufacturing SOPs).
    Deployed self-hosted on 2x g5.xlarge instances per region to avoid
    API-call latency and for data residency compliance.

  - Chunking strategy (now formalised in the internal Chunking Policy
    version 3.1):
      * Unstructured.io pre-processing for PDFs, DOCX, XLSX, HTML, MSG,
        PPTX
      * Primary strategy: Recursive character chunking with chunk_size =
        900 tokens, chunk_overlap = 120 tokens.
      * Secondary (domain-aware): Semantic chunking based on BGE-M3
        similarity boundary detection, applied only to long-form technical
        manuals (Meiji MES SOPs) and Bond pricing research notes where
        structural integrity matters more than uniformity.
      * CSV and tabular data: Hierarchical table-recognising chunker from
        the Unstructured.io library, preserving row-group semantics.

  - Reranker: ColBERT-IR v2 (colbert-ir-v2) deployed as the primary
    reranker in Q4 FY2026, replacing the current interim reranker based
    on cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-12-v2. Expected to deliver a
    measured 6–9% improvement in RAG faithfulness on adversarial QA.
    Currently under load testing; target GA: 1 December 2026.

5.5 Agentic Copilot 1.1 — Planning Module
The CTO reported that Agentic Copilot v1.1, which introduces a dedicated
planning module combining ReAct prompting with Tree of Thoughts (ToT)
branch-and-prune exploration for complex multi-step tasks, was currently
in final QA stage. She shared benchmark results on the 300-task DBizLabs

  - Task completion rate (v1.1, QA build 58): 79.6% (+11.3 percentage

  - Cost per task (LLM tokens): -4.3% compared to v1.0 (due to better
