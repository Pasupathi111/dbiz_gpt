# DBizLabs Q3 FY2026 Financial Report
*Reporting Period: 1 July 2026 – 30 September 2026*

**Headquarters:** Marina One West Tower, Level 22, 10 Marina Boulevard, Singapore 018961

## 1. Executive Summary

DBizLabs delivered a strong third quarter in FY2026 with total revenue reaching S$18.42 million, representing a 28.6% year-over-year increase compared to Q3 FY2025 (S$14.33 million). Growth was driven by accelerated enterprise adoption across all three flagship products: Meiji MES, Bond Copilot, and Hirethm HR SaaS.

Gross profit margin expanded to 71.4%, up 180 basis points from 69.6% in the prior year period, reflecting improved SaaS leverage and disciplined cost management across professional services delivery. EBITDA came in at S$3.81 million (20.7% margin), compared to S$2.52 million (17.6% margin) in Q3 FY2025, demonstrating meaningful operating leverage as the business scales.

Operating cash flow (OCF) was robust at S$4.22 million, translating to an OCF-to-EBITDA conversion ratio of 110.7%. Cash and cash equivalents on the balance sheet stood at S$23.6 million as of 30 September 2026, providing approximately 22 months of runway at the current operating expenditure run-rate. Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) improved to 68 days from 74 days in Q2 FY2026, reflecting enhanced collections discipline and favourable contract payment terms negotiated during the quarter.

The company added 5 new customers with annual contract value (ACV) exceeding S$1.0 million during the quarter, bringing the total count of million-plus customers to 23 as of quarter-end. Geographically, APAC remained the dominant region at 54.8% of revenue, followed by EMEA (24.9%) and the Americas (20.3%), reflecting the company's balanced international expansion strategy.

"We are very pleased with our Q3 performance, which underscores the durability of our AI-embedded product suite and the strength of our go-to-market motion across all three regions," said Siva Pasupathi, CEO of DBizLabs. "Meiji MES continues to take share in industrial manufacturing AI, Bond Copilot is now a standard tool in fixed-income trading desks across eight enterprise logos, and Hirethm is emerging as the HR AI platform of choice for mid-to-large APAC enterprises."

Revenue contribution by product line for Q3 FY2026:

Meiji MES maintained its position as the largest revenue contributor, driven by plant-wide deployments with Japanese and Southeast Asian manufacturing conglomerates. The OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) module and AI-powered predictive quality analytics add-on were the strongest sub-components within Meiji, representing 38% and 27% of Meiji revenue respectively.

The Audit & Compliance Pack add-on saw particularly strong uptake, with a 62% attach rate among new Bond Copilot enterprise customers during the quarter.

Hirethm HR SaaS grew 32.9% YoY, driven by wins in the public sector and financial services verticals. The newly launched ATS v2 module and AI-powered candidate shortlisting feature contributed to a 14.3% average ACV uplift for new Hirethm deals closed in Q3.

Professional Services revenue grew 12.0% YoY, a deliberate deceleration as the company continues to transition towards product-led growth with self-service onboarding and implementation accelerators. Training & Certification revenue increased 18.5% YoY, supported by the launch of DBiz Academy's Bond Copilot Certified Administrator programme in August 2026.

## 3. Profitability & Margins

### 3.1 Gross Profit and Gross Margin

Gross profit in Q3 FY2026 was S$13.15 million, representing a gross margin of 71.4%, compared to S$9.98 million (69.6% gross margin) in Q3 FY2025. The 180 basis point improvement was driven by:

- Higher SaaS revenue mix (87.4% vs. 84.8% in Q3 FY2025), carrying gross margins of 78–82%
- Improved utilisation rates in ProServe services, with billable utilisation averaging 81.3% vs. 76.9% in the prior year
- Optimised cloud infrastructure costs, with AWS and GCP spend per customer down 9.2% YoY following FinOps initiatives launched in Q1 FY2026

By product line, gross margins were: Meiji MES 73.8%, Bond Copilot 76.2%, Hirethm 74.5%, ProServe 42.1%, and Training 58.3%.

### 3.2 Operating Expenses

| Category | Q3 FY2026 (S$m) | % of Revenue | Q3 FY2025 (S$m) | YoY Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Research & Development | 4.52 | 24.5% | 3.41 | +32.6% |
| Sales & Marketing | 3.34 | 18.1% | 2.79 | +19.7% |
| General & Administrative | 1.48 | 8.0% | 1.26 | +17.5% |
| **Total Opex** | **9.34** | **50.7%** | **7.46** | **+25.2%** |

Total operating expenses grew 25.2% YoY, which was 340 basis points below revenue growth, demonstrating strong operating leverage. R&D investment continued to accelerate at +32.6% YoY, reflecting strategic investments in the Agentic Copilot platform, domain-specific RAG infrastructure, and multi-region data residency capabilities.

### 3.3 EBITDA

Earnings Before Interest, Tax, Depreciation, and Amortisation (EBITDA) for Q3 FY2026 was S$3.81 million, representing an EBITDA margin of 20.7%. This compares to S$2.52 million (17.6% margin) in Q3 FY2025. The improvement reflects the benefit of gross margin expansion and disciplined opex management, with the adjusted EBITDA margin (excluding S$0.18 million of one-time JV advisory costs) standing at 21.7%.

## 4. Cash Flow & Balance Sheet

### 4.1 Operating Cash Flow

Operating cash flow was S$4.22 million in Q3 FY2026, compared to S$3.01 million in Q3 FY2025. The conversion ratio of OCF-to-EBITDA was 110.7%, benefiting from:

- S$1.21 million of advance payments received for Q4 and FY2027 contracts
- S$0.64 million of receivables collected in excess of revenue during the month of September
- Improved vendor payment terms with key cloud infrastructure providers (net-45 vs. net-30 prior)

Capital expenditure (Capex) for the quarter was S$1.07 million, comprising S$0.72 million of cloud capacity expansion and infrastructure investments, S$0.23 million of internal-use software development costs, and S$0.12 million of office equipment and leasehold improvements. Free cash flow (OCF less Capex) was S$3.15 million, representing a free cash flow margin of 17.1%.

### 4.2 Cash Position and Liquidity

Cash and cash equivalents as at 30 September 2026 were S$23.6 million, compared to S$19.4 million at the end of Q2 FY2026 and S$15.8 million at the end of Q3 FY2025. The increase reflects strong OCF generation of S$4.22 million plus S$0.34 million of government R&D grants received during the quarter.

The company maintains a S$50.0 million commercial paper shelf programme (undrawn) with DBS Bank, OCBC Bank, and United Overseas Bank, providing additional liquidity headroom. Total debt outstanding was S$nil as at quarter-end, reflecting the company's net-cash balance-sheet position.

## 6. FX Impact & Macroeconomics

The strengthening of the Singapore Dollar against the US Dollar during Q3 FY2026 (SGD/USD moved from 0.729 at quarter start to 0.746 at quarter end, representing a +2.3% move) had a net positive impact of S$0.12 million on reported revenue. Approximately 58% of DBizLabs revenue is denominated in USD, 26% in SGD, 10% in EUR, and 6% in JPY and GBP combined.

The company currently hedges approximately 55% of its forecast USD-denominated receivables on a rolling 6-month basis using forward contracts, with an additional S$10.0 million of option-based collars protecting against adverse SGD/USD movements below 0.715 or above 0.765.

A sensitivity analysis on unhedged exposures indicates that a 1% movement in SGD/USD has an approximate S$42,000 impact on quarterly EBITDA. For EUR/SGD, a 1% move is equivalent to approximately S$18,000 of EBITDA impact.

## 8. Risk Factors

Investors should carefully consider the following risk factors, together with all other information included in this report, before making any investment decisions relating to DBizLabs securities.

### 8.1 Market and Competitive Risks

The enterprise AI platform market is characterised by rapid technological change, evolving customer requirements, and intense competition. Competitors include both well-established enterprise software vendors (e.g. ServiceNow, Salesforce Einstein, Microsoft Copilot for 365) and a large number of well-funded venture-backed start-ups. There can be no assurance that DBizLabs will be able to maintain its competitive position, continue to win share in its target verticals, or avoid pricing pressure on its core product offerings.

### 8.2 Technology and Product Risks

The company's growth strategy is materially dependent on the successful development and commercialisation of Agentic Copilot, the next-generation AI platform built on the Open WebUI fork. Delays in product roadmap delivery, quality issues with AI-generated outputs, or failure to achieve competitive performance benchmarks (e.g. RAG faithfulness scores, tool-use success rates) could materially impact customer retention, new customer acquisition, and revenue growth.

### 8.3 Regulatory and Compliance Risks

The regulatory environment for AI systems is evolving rapidly, with the Singapore AI Governance Act (effective Q1 2027), the EU AI Act (effective 2026–2028 phased implementation), and potential analogous legislation in the US, UK, and other jurisdictions. Compliance with new regulatory requirements may require material investment, may restrict the use of certain AI techniques, or may delay product launches in certain jurisdictions. Failure to comply with applicable regulations could result in significant fines, reputational damage, or restrictions on the company's ability to operate.

### 8.4 Concentration and Customer Risks

As at 30 September 2026, the company's top 5 customers represented approximately 31.4% of trailing twelve-month revenue, and the top 10 customers represented approximately 47.8%. The loss of one or more significant customers, or a significant reduction in spend by such customers, could have a material adverse effect on the company's results of operations and financial condition.

### 8.5 Foreign Exchange and Macroeconomic Risks

Given the company's international revenue base, fluctuations in foreign exchange rates (particularly SGD/USD, SGD/EUR, and SGD/JPY) can have a meaningful impact on reported revenue and profitability. Additionally, a prolonged global economic slowdown, tightening of IT spending budgets by enterprise customers, or adverse conditions in the manufacturing, financial services, or HR technology sectors could materially impact demand for the company's products and services.

### 8.6 Cybersecurity and Data Privacy Risks

The company processes significant volumes of sensitive customer data, including manufacturing quality data, bond pricing and trade information, HR records, and personally identifiable information (PII). A material cybersecurity incident, data breach, or failure to comply with applicable data privacy legislation (including Singapore's PDPA, EU GDPR, and California's CCPA/CPRA) could expose the company to significant regulatory fines, customer claims, reputational harm, and loss of business.
